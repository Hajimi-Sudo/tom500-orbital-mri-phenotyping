# Frontier Attention Evidence

## Academic signals

- TED orbital MRI radiomics has already been used to predict intravenous
  glucocorticoid response in a 127-patient cohort, including multi-region
  orbital features. This confirms active methodological competition and means
  a simple radiomics classifier needs a sharper clinical question.
- A 2025 public TOM500 dataset paper reports 500 annotated TED orbital MRI cases,
  with T2WI and nine orbital structures. This may be the actual accessible
  resource behind the proposed direction, but its reported clinical variables
  do not establish the claimed CAS/TSH/FT4/TRAb package.
- Recent TAO MRI work continues to examine activity, treatment response, and
  quantitative imaging, so novelty should come from endocrine linkage,
  incremental clinical value, and rigorous validation rather than radiomics
  extraction alone.

## Official journal signal

The *Frontiers in Endocrinology* Thyroid Endocrinology section explicitly covers
human thyroid diseases, therapy and treatment outcomes, and thyroid
pathophysiology. TAO is therefore in scope when the manuscript foregrounds its
relationship to thyroid autoimmunity and endocrine management.

## Scope risk

The direction is relevant but not low-competition. Existing TED MRI radiomics
papers reduce the novelty of a basic active/inactive classifier. The study
should not be promoted until the dataset is verified and the manuscript claim
is narrowed to endocrine-imaging association and incremental value.
